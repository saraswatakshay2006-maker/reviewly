---

## 1. Executive Summary

The platform is a **Customer Trust & Experience Technology company**, not a "cheap NFC card vendor." The physical NFC card
is the *entry point*; the real product is the multi-tenant SaaS ecosystem behind it — beautiful customer landing pages,
a compliant Google-review redirection path, private feedback, analytics, and a professional business dashboard.

The core promise: **"Make every genuine customer experience easier to share."**

Three parties win:

- **Business owner** — collects genuine feedback, simplifies the review path, understands the customer experience.
- **Customer** — enjoys a frictionless, transparent, attractive feedback experience.
- **Platform brand** — becomes a trusted infrastructure provider recognized by both customers and businesses.

**Why now / why this will work:** incumbents in the Indian NFC-review space compete on hardware price and — critically —
many lean on **review gating**, **"shield against negative feedback"** and **aggressive personal-data capture**
(verified in research). This platform explicitly does the opposite: a transparent, disinterested, trust-first journey
with minimal data collection. That is a genuine, defensible differentiation.

> Constraints honored throughout (from the master PRD, never relaxed): no review gating; no selective redirection; no
> sentiment-based restrictions; equal access to Google option for all ratings; no fabricated reviews; no fake praise;
> no unsupported SEO keywords; no forced text; no claim of "review submitted"; no bypassing of Google's review composer;
> no founder branding implying Google verification/endorsement; a subtle platform signature that never overpowers the
> client's brand; QR = same functionality as NFC.

## 2. Product Vision

Build a platform that makes sharing genuine customer experiences **incredibly easy, natural, convenient and trustworthy.**

The customer should not feel forced to leave a review. The experience should make them *genuinely interested* in
sharing feedback.

**Vision statement:** A world where every great customer experience is naturally, transparently, and easily shared —
where businesses learn from real feedback, and where the platform is a recognized symbol of *trustworthy* customer
feedback.

## 3. Problem Statement

1. **Businesses lose reviews they deserve.** Customers rarely navigate Google to write a review after a good visit;
   friction kills intent.
2. **Existing solutions break trust.** Many competitors gate negative feedback, "shield" businesses from low ratings,
   or harvest customer names/phones/birthdays without clarity — violating good practice and eroding the very trust that
   reviews are supposed to build.
3. **Hardware-first players under-deliver on software.** Cheap NFC/QR cards exist, but the *software ecosystem*
   (dashboard, analytics, multi-tenant accounts) is thin.
4. **Global platforms are heavy and expensive.** Podium/Birdeye-class suites are US-centric, quote-based, and built for
   mid-market; they are poor fits for Indian single-location SMBs at ₹-scale pricing.
5. **No recognizable, transparent brand** currently owns "customer experience technology" in India's review space.

## 7. Product Differentiation

1. **The anti-gating stance as a feature.** Unlike competitors who "shield" businesses, the platform treats every rating
   (1–5) equally. This protects the business's long-term reputation, satisfies Google's review-manipulation policies,
   and is a marketing message we can state loudly and honestly.
2. **Minimal data, maximal trust.** No names/phones/birthdays harvested silently. Private feedback is optional and
   disclosed transparently.
3. **Software-first.** The NFC card is a trigger; the value is the landing page + dashboard + AI-assist.
4. **A real brand.** A deliberate visual language across card, QR stand, landing page, dashboard, emails.
5. **Compliance as a differentiator.** Documented adherence to Google Maps UGC / Business Profile policies and DPDP
   principles — rare in the Indian segment.

## 8. User Journeys

### Journey A — NFC tap (happy path)
1. Customer sees a premium branded NFC card at counter/reception/checkout.
2. Taps phone. **Browser opens `https://platform/r/<business-slug>`** (unique URL).
3. Landing page loads fast (mobile-first): business logo, name, category, welcoming message, short "why honest feedback
   matters" line, and a clear platform signature ("Powered by [Platform]").
4. Customer selects 1–5 stars. Page responds warmly ("Thank you for sharing your experience.").
5. Two options appear, **both always available regardless of rating**:
   - **Continue to Google Reviews** → opens the business's official Google write-a-review URL (no claim of submission, no
     bypass of Google's composer).
   - **Share private feedback** (optional) → opens a short form.
6. Customer writes independently, or uses **optional, clearly-labeled AI assistance** (see §12), edits freely, and
   decides. On Google, the customer submits directly in Google's composer.

### Journey B — QR fallback (same functionality)
Same as A, but device has no NFC: customer scans the printed QR (placed on card face/stand) and lands on the *same*
URL with identical functionality.

### Journey C — Business onboarding
Owner signs up → verifies → registers business (name, category, address) → uploads logo → pastes/modifies **Google
Business Profile review link** → system generates a unique public URL, NFC token(s), and QR code(s) → order/prep cards.

## 9. Functional Requirements

Priorities: **P0** (MVP must-have), **P1** (shortly after MVP), **P2** (later).

### 9.1 Customer landing page (P0)
- **Purpose:** turn a tap/scan into an engaging, transparent feedback experience.
- **User story:** *As a customer, I want to leave feedback quickly and clearly, without feeling trapped or harvested.*
- **Functional behaviour:** server-rendered mobile-first page; business brand hero; 1–5 star selector; equal access to
  Google option at every rating; optional private feedback; platform signature.
- **Edge cases:** slow connection (weight budget, SSR/static); very long business names; missing logo; scripts/JS
  disabled (progressive enhancement); repeated sessions.
- **Dependencies:** business settings, storage, analytics events.
- **Acceptance criteria:** loads <2.5s on 3G mid-tier; every rating shows both options; signature never overpowers brand.
- **Priority:** P0.

### 9.2 Interactive rating (P0)
- **Purpose:** capture the customer's experience in one tap while keeping the path honest.
- **User story:** *As a customer, I can rate my experience from 1 to 5 and I am never funneled differently based on my rating.*
- **Behaviour:** five selectable stars; after selection → "Thank you for sharing your experience." transition, then both
  actions.
- **Edge cases:** partial tap, cancel, double-tap, animation on slow devices.
- **Acceptance:** all five ratings receive the same two options; no gating logic anywhere.
- **Priority:** P0.

### 9.3 Google review redirection (P0)
- **Purpose:** let customers leave a Google review with zero friction — without the platform touching Google.
- **User story:** *As a customer, I tap "Share Your Experience on Google" and land on the business's real Google review page.*
- **Behaviour:** button opens the correct official Google write-a-review URL in a new tab; the platform **never claims
  the review was submitted**, never auto-submits, never alters Google's composer.
- **Edge cases:** missing/invalid review link configured by the owner; link changes; blocked popups; slow navigation.
- **Dependencies:** business_settings.google_review_url (validated).
- **Acceptance:** URL is the owner-configured official link; no submission claim anywhere; link-click documented as a
  *click*, never as a submitted review.
- **Priority:** P0.

### 9.4 Private feedback (P0)
- **Purpose:** capture unresolvable-by-Google details (optional, transparent).
- **User story:** *As a customer, I may optionally tell the business what they could improve, without being required to.*
- **Behaviour:** optional free-text form; labeled clearly as private and only visible to the business; **no personal data
  is required — the MVP form collects free text only and exposes no contact (email/phone) fields**; submissions are posted
  to the business's feedback inbox.
- **Edge cases:** empty submit disabled; spam/abuse throttling; long input; profanity (optional filter, P1).
- **Acceptance:** opt-in only; no mandatory PII required; the MVP collects no contact PII in private feedback; inbox
  restricted to authorized business members. If optional contact fields are ever added in a later release, they are
  treated as personal data under DPDP (see §17) with explicit consent, purpose limitation, retention limits, and
  anonymization/deletion options.
- **Priority:** P0.

### 9.5 AI-assisted review writing (P0/P1)
- **Purpose:** help customers write clearer reviews *without fabricating content*.
- **User story:** *As a customer, I can ask for help to phrase my own experience, keep my meaning, and either use or discard the suggestion.*
- **Behaviour:** customer voluntarily describes service/positives/improvements. The AI improves grammar/clarity/readability
  **preserving the customer's actual meaning**. Suggestion is **clearly labeled as AI-assisted**; editable; deletable;
  never required; never auto-fills; never writes the review for the customer on Google; nothing is ever injected without
  the customer's explicit decision.
- **Hard constraints:** never fabricate experiences; never generate fake praise; never insert unsupported SEO keywords;
  never force suggested text; empty-text option always available.
- **Edge cases:** ambiguous input; off-topic input; very short input; refusal to assist with sensitive content.
- **Dependencies:** LLM provider (server-side, key-protected), rate limiting.
- **Acceptance:** every suggestion is editable/deletable and labeled AI; no auto-publish anywhere.
- **Priority:** P0 (MVP can ship basic version); P1 for richer UX.

### 9.6 Business dashboard (P0)
- **Purpose:** give owners a professional multi-tenant view of their business's performance.
- **User story:** *As a business owner, I see my scans, rating interactions, Google-button clicks, and private feedback, and I can distinguish clicks from published reviews.*
- **Behaviour:** overview metrics (NFC scans, QR scans, unique page visits where privacy rules permit, rating
  interactions, Google-button clicks, private feedback submissions, device breakdown, daily/weekly/monthly activity);
  **Google-button clicks are clearly labeled as clicks, never as published reviews**; explicit note that published
  review counts are only shown if an official, authorized source provides them.
- **Edge cases:** zero data empty states; scale (many events); timezone handling.
- **Dependencies:** analytics pipeline, RLS.
- **Acceptance:** metrics computed from first-party events; distinct metric for link-clicks vs (future) published reviews.
- **Priority:** P0.

### 9.7 Business management (P0)
- **Purpose:** self-service registration and configuration.
- **Behaviour:** register business; name/address/category; logo upload; Google review link (validated); customize landing
  page (brand color/logo/welcome message); manage NFC card URLs; generate QR codes; manage branches (P1); update info.
- **Edge cases:** invalid Google URL (validate format/host); logo format/size; duplicate business.
- **Acceptance:** all configured values appear on the landing page; validation blocks malformed URLs.
- **Priority:** P0.

### 9.8 Feedback management (P0)
- **Purpose:** private inbox with triage.
- **Behaviour:** list feedback; categorize; date filter; search; export (CSV); satisfaction analytics; optional response
  note; complaint tracking flag. Access restricted to authorized members (RLS).
- **Edge cases:** large export; empty inbox; attachments (P2).
- **Acceptance:** data isolated per tenant; export honors filters.
- **Priority:** P0.

### 9.9 NFC card management (P0/P1)
- **Purpose:** manage physical cards as part of the ecosystem.
- **Behaviour:** each card gets a unique secure token (`platform/t/<card-id>`); cards tied to a business/branch; redirect
  management without rewriting tags (changing the business's Google destination does **not** require reflashing cards);
  card activation/replacement/link reassignment; NFC compatibility notes; inventory module (P2) for bulk manufacturing.
- **Edge cases:** lost card; reassigned card; duplicate token; hardware variants (NTAG213/215).
- **Acceptance:** token→destination resolution is server-side; never rewrite the NFC payload to change destination.
- **Priority:** P0 (core), P1 (replacement/inventory).

### 9.10 Auth & multi-tenancy (P0)
- **Purpose:** independent accounts and strict isolation.
- **Behaviour:** roles = Super Admin, Business Owner, Branch Manager, Staff. Tenant-level data isolation; RLS; RBAC;
  secure auth; rate limiting; audit logging; input validation; backups/recovery.
- **Acceptance:** one business can never read another's data (proven by RLS + tests).
- **Priority:** P0.

### 9.11 Analytics (P0)
- **Purpose:** privacy-conscious first-party event tracking.
- **Behaviour:** events: page_view, rating_select, google_click, feedback_submit, scan (nfc/qr). Aggregations;
  device breakdown; no third-party ad cookies; no cross-site tracking.
- **Acceptance:** aggregated without PII; distinct click-vs-published metric.
- **Priority:** P0.

## 10. Non-Functional Requirements

- **Performance:** landing page TTI <2.5s on mid-tier 3G; dashboard <2s.
- **Availability/Reliability:** ≥99.9% target; graceful degradation if the LLM/analytics is down (page still works).
- **Scalability:** multi-tenant; horizontal scaling of APIs; DB connection pooling; caching of public landing pages.
- **Security:** HTTPS everywhere; secure session cookies; RBAC + RLS; input validation; rate limiting; audit log; secret
  management (LLM key, DB creds); no PII in URLs/logs.
- **Privacy:** minimal data; DPDP-aligned; clear consent where personal data is processed; data retention limits.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard navigable stars, visible focus.
- **Mobile:** responsive-first; touch-optimized.
- **Localization:** English first; Hinglish + regional languages later (P1/P2).

## 11. UI/UX Specifications

- **Avoid:** generic AI-generated dashboards; fake statistics; overcrowding.
- **Platform signature:** subtle footer chip on every customer page: small lock/check mark + *"Powered by [Platform
  Name]"* + *"A simple, transparent way to share your genuine experience."* — unobtrusive, never overpowers the
  client's brand, never implies Google verification.

## 12. AI-Assisted Review — Trust & Compliance Detail

- Suggestion is generated server-side **only after the customer actively asks** and provides their own words.
- The prompt is constrained: *improve grammar/clarity/readability; preserve meaning; do not add praise the customer did
  not express; do not add SEO keywords; do not add claims about the business.*
- Output is presented in an editable box with the label **"AI-assisted suggestion"**; the customer can edit, discard, or
  use it. Nothing is posted anywhere without the customer's own action.
- No timers, autosubmit, or "recommended text" forced flows.

## 16. Security Architecture

- Supabase Auth (email/OTP or magic link; later social). JWT + session.
- **RLS policies on every tenant table** (owner/branch-manager/staff scoped).
- RBAC matrix (Super Admin / Owner / Branch Manager / Staff).
- Rate limiting on auth, public tokens, AI assist, feedback submit.
- Input validation (Zod) on all boundaries; safe redirect allow-listing (only validated Google review hosts).
- NFC/QR tokens are high-entropy UUIDs; tokens are not guessable; token→destination resolved server-side.
- Audit logging for privileged actions.
- Secrets in env/vault; never in client bundle.
- Backups (Supabase PITR/standard) + tested recovery.

## 17. Compliance & Legal

- **Google Maps UGC & Business Profile policies:** no incentivized/fake reviews; no gating; no autoposting; no
  unauthorized Google API usage; no claim of Google endorsement/verification. We never represent that the platform
  verifies reviews or that Google has endorsed the product. Google *write-a-review* links are owner-configured, official,
  and we only open them.
- **DPDP Act (India):** the platform minimizes personal data and **collects no mandatory PII** from customers; the MVP
  customer journey (rating, Google redirection, private feedback) works fully without any contact information. Where any
  personal data is processed (e.g., if optional contact fields are introduced in a future release, or business-owner
  account data), the platform will maintain a lawful basis and transparency, obtain explicit consent with a recorded
  consent trail, apply purpose limitation and defined retention limits (with deletion/anonymization on request), and
  remain breach-ready as required under the DPDP Act.
- **Explicit prohibitions:** review gating; incentivized positive reviews; fake reviews; manipulation; suppression of
  negative reviews; automated review submission; unauthorized Google API usage; misleading Google affiliation claims.
- **Privacy center:** clearly written policy on the marketing site and in-app.
